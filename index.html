Sunday Batch-Cook → Easy Week
  • Cook big pots of quinoa + brown rice; portion & store (fridge 5 days / freeze longer).
  • Brown large batches of ground beef + ground turkey with the garlic-oil base; split across meals.
  • Pre-chop the week's veg into one container.
  • Keep salmon + chicken frozen; thaw the morning of.
  • Weeknight = reheat protein + grain, quick-sauté veg (10–15 min).
Flavor Base (no onion / garlic)
Garlic-INFUSED olive oil + scallion GREEN tops + fresh ginger + lemon/lime + herbs + salt. Infused oil is low-FODMAP because FODMAPs aren't oil-soluble — this replaces onion & garlic everywhere.
Iron Boost
Pair iron foods (beef, spinach, pumpkin seeds) with vitamin C (bell pepper, tomato, citrus) in the same meal to maximize absorption.
