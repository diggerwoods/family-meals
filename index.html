Prep
Built around Mom's targets; everyone else can add extras on the side. Use any 3–5 per week. Scaled for a hungry family of 5 with leftovers.
1. Beef & Veggie Rice BowlsIRON
Protein: ~2.5–3 lb ground beef (85/15). Brown in garlic-infused oil; season with ginger, scallion GREEN tops, tamari, salt. Base: brown rice or quinoa. Veg: zucchini, carrots, spinach, red bell pepper.
Why it works: beef + spinach + bell pepper (vit C) = max iron. Batch & freeze the beef-veg mix.
2. Sheet-Pan Lemon-Herb Chicken
Protein: chicken thighs (bone-in = juicy + cheap). Veg: carrots, zucchini, potato, bell pepper. Toss in garlic-infused oil, lemon, rosemary/thyme, salt. Roast 400°F ~35 min. One pan, great leftovers.
3. Baked Salmon + QuinoaOMEGA-3
Protein: 5–6 salmon portions, olive oil + lemon + dill + salt, bake 400°F 12–15 min. Sides: quinoa + green beans. Fast (~25 min).
4. Turkey & Zucchini Meatball Skillet
Protein: ~2.5 lb ground turkey + egg + GF breadcrumb (or almond flour) + herbs. Sear meatballs; simmer in PLAIN crushed tomato + garlic-infused oil + basil. Serve over quinoa or zucchini noodles. Freezes great.
5. Beef & Veggie Stir-FryIRON · FAST
Protein: thin-sliced sirloin/flank, stir-fried in garlic-infused oil with ginger, bok choy, carrots, bell pepper, scallion tops, tamari. Over brown rice. 15–20 min.
Easy Backups
Veggie frittata (eggs = protein + iron, add spinach + pepper) · lettuce/rice-paper wraps with leftover protein.

⚠️ Label-Reading Traps
Soy sauce → use TAMARI / certified GF (regular = wheat).
Canned tomato / sauce → PLAIN only (most have onion & garlic).
Broth / stock → almost all have onion & garlic — avoid or buy low-FODMAP.
"Seasoned" meats → usually onion/garlic powder — buy plain, season yourself.
Breadcrumbs → must be gluten-free (or use almond flour).
Sunday Batch-Cook → Easy Week
  • Cook a big pot of quinoa + a big pot of brown rice. Portion into containers (fridge 5 days / freeze longer).
  • Brown a large batch of ground beef and ground turkey with the garlic-oil + seasoning base. Split for Bowls (meal 1) and Meatballs (meal 4).
  • Pre-chop the week's veg (carrots, zucchini, bell pepper, green beans) into one big container.
  • Keep salmon + chicken thighs frozen; thaw the morning of.
  • Weeknight = reheat a protein + a grain, quick-sauté fresh veg. 10–15 min to plate.
Flavor Base (no onion/garlic)
Low-FODMAP flavor comes from garlic-INFUSED olive oil (FODMAPs aren't oil-soluble, so infused oil is safe) + scallion GREEN tops + fresh ginger + lemon/lime + herbs + salt. This single swap replaces onion & garlic in every recipe.
Iron Boost
Pair iron-rich foods (beef, spinach, pumpkin seeds) with vitamin C (bell pepper, tomato, citrus) in the same meal to maximize absorption.
